# Subscription Management

## Purpose

Manage the complete lifecycle of tenant subscriptions.

---

# Subscription Lifecycle

Trial

↓

Active

↓

Renewal Due

↓

Grace Period

↓

Suspended

↓

Expired

↓

Cancelled

---

# Subscription Information

Each subscription stores:

- Tenant
- Selected Plan
- Billing Cycle
- Activation Date
- Expiration Date
- Renewal Date
- Current Status

---

# Billing Cycles

- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Semi-Annual
- Annual

---

# Automatic Renewal

Supported when automatic payment is enabled.

---

# Grace Period

Schools continue accessing the platform during the configured grace period.

Default

7 days

---

# Suspension

Subscriptions are suspended automatically when:

- Payment fails
- Grace period expires
- Fraud is detected

---

# Reactivation

Occurs automatically after successful payment.

---

# Upgrade

Immediate activation.

Remaining subscription value is prorated.

---

# Downgrade

Applied at the next renewal.

---

# Cancellation

Schools may cancel subscriptions at any time.

Historical data remains preserved according to the retention policy.

---

# Notifications

Automatic reminders are sent:

- 30 days before expiration
- 7 days before expiration
- 3 days before expiration
- On expiration
- During grace period

---

# Audit

All subscription events are recorded.
