# Subscription Plans

## Purpose

Define the subscription plans available to educational institutions using Nexus School.

---

# Objectives

- Provide flexible pricing.
- Support institutions of different sizes.
- Enable feature-based access.
- Allow future plan expansion.

---

# Available Plans

## Starter

Designed for small schools.

Features

- Student Management
- Teacher Management
- Attendance
- Academic Management
- Basic Reports
- Email Support

---

## Professional

Designed for growing schools.

Includes everything in Starter plus:

- Finance
- Library
- Transport
- Hostel
- Parent Portal
- Mobile Application
- Advanced Reports

---

## Enterprise

Designed for universities and large institutions.

Includes everything in Professional plus:

- Multi-campus Management
- API Access
- AI Features
- Custom Branding
- Custom Domain
- Priority Support
- Dedicated Account Manager

---

# Feature Availability

Features are enabled according to the subscribed plan.

---

# Upgrade

Schools may upgrade at any time.

Upgrades take effect immediately after payment confirmation.

---

# Downgrade

Downgrades become effective at the next billing cycle unless otherwise specified.

---

# Custom Plans

Enterprise customers may receive custom pricing and feature packages.

---

# Future Enhancements

- Annual discounts
