# Business Rules

## Purpose

Document the core business rules governing the Nexus School platform.

---

# General Rules

- Every school belongs to exactly one tenant.
- Every user belongs to one tenant.
- Data isolation between tenants is mandatory.

---

# Academic Rules

- One active Academic Year per institution.
- Students cannot have duplicate active enrollments.
- Subjects belong to a defined curriculum.

---

# Financial Rules

- Paid invoices cannot be modified.
- Refunds require approval.
- Outstanding balances affect eligibility where configured.

---

# Attendance Rules

- One attendance record per person per session.
- Approved attendance cannot be edited without authorization.

---

# Assessment Rules

- Grades follow approved grading scales.
- Published results become read-only unless reopened.

---

# Subscription Rules

- Active subscription required for platform access.
- Expired subscriptions enter the grace period before suspension.

---

# Security Rules

- Every action requires authorization.
- Sensitive operations are audited.
- Tenant isolation is always enforced.
