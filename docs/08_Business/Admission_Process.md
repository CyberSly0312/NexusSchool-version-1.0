# Admission Process

## Purpose

Define the standardized admission workflow for educational institutions using Nexus School.

---

# Objectives

- Standardize admissions
- Improve transparency
- Support configurable workflows
- Maintain complete audit history

---

# Admission Workflow

1. Application Submitted

2. Document Verification

3. Entrance Examination (Optional)

4. Interview (Optional)

5. Evaluation

6. Admission Decision

7. Admission Offer

8. Acceptance by Applicant

9. Fee Payment

10. Student Registration

11. Enrollment

---

# Required Documents

Examples

- Birth Certificate
- Previous School Report
- National ID (where applicable)
- Passport Photograph
- Medical Record (optional)

---

# Admission Status

- Draft
- Submitted
- Under Review
- Interview Scheduled
- Accepted
- Rejected
- Enrolled

---

# Notifications

Automatic notifications are sent during every important stage.

---

# Audit

Every admission decision is recorded with full history.
