# 4. Architectural Decisions

The following strategic decisions were confirmed.

## Governance First

The project shall maintain enterprise-grade governance documentation throughout its lifecycle.

Documentation is considered a software deliverable.

---

## Documentation Before Expansion

No new governance documents will be created unless they provide measurable long-term value.

Future work shall focus on operational documentation and business implementation.

---

## Project Continuity

Every engineering session shall

* begin with SESSION_HANDOFF.md;
* review CURRENT_POSITION.md when necessary;
* update the Project Journal;
* conclude with updated operational documentation.

This workflow ensures continuity across developers and AI assistants.

# 7. Key Discussions

Several important long-term product ideas were identified.

## Academic Communication

Official documents shall be rendered exclusively by the Academic Communication Engine.

---

## Records Lifecycle

Historical academic records shall remain immutable and follow a controlled lifecycle.

---

## Cloud Archive

Future versions of NexusSchool shall support

* downloadable archive packages;
* tenant archive lifecycle management;
* configurable retention policies;
* cloud object storage integration;
* paid archive storage expansion.

This functionality is planned as a dedicated platform service rather than a simple file repository.

---

## Academic Council Publications

The Academic Communication Engine shall support publication of official Class Council minutes.

Visibility shall be configurable for

* Directors
* Teachers
* Parents
* Students

Publication decisions remain under school administration control.
