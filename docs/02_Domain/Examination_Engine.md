# Examination Engine

## Purpose

Coordinates examination planning, execution, and result production.

---

## Responsibilities

- Schedule examinations
- Allocate examination rooms
- Generate seating plans
- Record attendance
- Publish examination sessions

---

## Inputs

- Subjects
- Timetable
- Students
- Teachers

---

## Outputs

- Examination Schedule
- Attendance
- Examination Results

---

## Business Rules

- No timetable conflicts.
- Student eligibility verified.
- Examination integrity enforced.

---

## Produced Events

- ExaminationScheduled
- ExaminationStarted
- ExaminationCompleted

---

## Security

- Secure examination process
- Audit all examination activities

---

## Future Improvements

- CBT integration
- Online examinations
