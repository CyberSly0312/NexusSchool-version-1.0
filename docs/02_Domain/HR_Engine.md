# HR Engine

## Purpose

Manages the complete employee lifecycle.

---

## Responsibilities

- Staff recruitment
- Employee management
- Contracts
- Leave management
- Payroll coordination
- Performance evaluation

---

## Inputs

- Employee Profiles
- Contracts
- Attendance
- Payroll Data

---

## Outputs

- Employee Records
- Leave Reports
- Performance Reports

---

## Business Rules

- One active contract.
- Leave approval workflow.
- Payroll validation required.

---

## Produced Events

- EmployeeHired
- EmployeePromoted
- LeaveApproved

---

## Future Improvements

- AI recruitment
- Performance analytics
