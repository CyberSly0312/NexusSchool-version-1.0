# Transport Engine

## Purpose

Coordinates school transportation services.

---

## Responsibilities

- Route management
- Vehicle assignment
- Driver allocation
- Student assignment
- Transport billing

---

## Inputs

- Student Profiles
- Routes
- Vehicles
- Drivers

---

## Outputs

- Route Plans
- Passenger Lists
- Transport Reports

---

## Business Rules

- Vehicle capacity enforced.
- Driver license validation.
- Route optimization.

---

## Produced Events

- RouteAssigned
- StudentBoarded
- StudentDropped

---

## Future Improvements

- GPS tracking
- Parent notifications
- Live vehicle location
