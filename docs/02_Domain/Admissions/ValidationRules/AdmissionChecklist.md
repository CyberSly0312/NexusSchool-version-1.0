# Admission Checklist Validation Rules

## Purpose

Defines validation rules for Admission Checklists.

---

## Required Fields

- Admission Application
- Checklist Template
- Responsible Officer

---

## Validation Rules

- All mandatory Checklist Items must be completed.
- Financial Clearance must be verified.
- Medical Clearance must be verified when required.
- Required Documents must be approved.
- Decision must be Published before final approval.

---

## Business Constraints

- Approved Checklists cannot be modified.
- Enrollment requires an Approved Checklist.
- Archived Checklists remain immutable.

---

## Error Messages

- Checklist incomplete.
- Outstanding Financial Clearance.
- Missing Medical Clearance.
- Required Documents not verified.
- Admission Decision not published.
