# Admission Decision Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow defines how final Admission Decisions are produced.

---

# Trigger

All admission evaluations have been completed.

---

# Preconditions

- Application verified.
- Required Documents approved.
- Interview completed (if applicable).
- Examination completed (if applicable).

---

# Workflow Steps

1. Collect Evaluation Results.
2. Verify Admission Requirements.
3. Calculate Overall Assessment.
4. Generate Recommendation.
5. Manager Reviews Recommendation.
6. Approve or Reject Decision.
7. Publish Decision.

---

# Status Flow

Pending Review

↓

Under Evaluation

↓

Approved

OR

Rejected

OR

Waitlisted

↓

Published

---

# Exceptions

- Missing Evaluation
- Missing Documents
- Manager Rejection
- Appeal Submitted

---

# Notifications

- Decision Approved
- Decision Rejected
- Waitlist Notification

---

# Result

Admission Decision is finalized.
