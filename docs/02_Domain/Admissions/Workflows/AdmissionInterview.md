# Admission Interview Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow defines the interview process used to assess Applicants during Admissions.

---

# Trigger

Admission Officer schedules an Interview.

---

# Preconditions

- Admission Application is verified.
- Applicant is eligible for Interview.
- Interview Panel is assigned.

---

# Workflow Steps

1. Schedule Interview.
2. Notify Applicant.
3. Assign Interview Panel.
4. Conduct Interview.
5. Record Evaluation Scores.
6. Record Interview Comments.
7. Submit Evaluation.
8. Manager reviews Interview.
9. Finalize Interview Result.

---

# Status Flow

Scheduled

↓

Confirmed

↓

In Progress

↓

Completed

↓

Reviewed

---

# Exceptions

- Applicant Absent
- Interview Cancelled
- Technical Failure
- Panel Unavailable

---

# Notifications

- Interview Scheduled
- Reminder Sent
- Interview Completed
- Evaluation Submitted

---

# Result

Interview Result becomes available for Admission Decision.
