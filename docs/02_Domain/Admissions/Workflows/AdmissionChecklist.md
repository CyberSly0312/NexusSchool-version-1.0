# Admission Checklist Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow ensures that all admission requirements and administrative validations are completed before Enrollment.

---

# Trigger

Admission Application reaches the final verification stage.

---

# Preconditions

- Admission Decision approved.
- Required Documents verified.
- Admission Offer accepted.

---

# Workflow Steps

1. Generate Admission Checklist.
2. Verify mandatory Documents.
3. Verify Admission Requirements.
4. Verify Interview completion (if applicable).
5. Verify Examination completion (if applicable).
6. Verify Financial Clearance.
7. Verify Medical Clearance (if required).
8. Complete Checklist.
9. Authorize Student creation.
10. Authorize Enrollment.

---

# Status Flow

Created

↓

In Progress

↓

Pending Verification

↓

Completed

↓

Approved

↓

Enrollment Authorized

---

# Exceptions

- Missing Documents
- Outstanding Fees
- Failed Medical Clearance
- Incomplete Evaluation
- Manual Override Required

---

# Notifications

- Checklist Created
- Checklist Updated
- Checklist Completed
- Enrollment Authorized

---

# Integrations

- Document Management
- Finance Module
- Medical Module
- Student Module
- Enrollment Module
- Notification Service

---

# Result

Applicant satisfies all admission requirements and is authorized to become a Student.
