# Admission Exam Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow manages Admission Examinations from scheduling to publication of results.

---

# Trigger

Applicant is scheduled for an Admission Examination.

---

# Preconditions

- Admission Application verified.
- Examination Session available.
- Applicant registered.

---

# Workflow Steps

1. Schedule Examination.
2. Register Applicant.
3. Notify Applicant.
4. Conduct Examination.
5. Submit Scripts or Responses.
6. Grade Examination.
7. Review Scores.
8. Publish Results.

---

# Status Flow

Scheduled

↓

Registered

↓

In Progress

↓

Completed

↓

Graded

↓

Published

---

# Exceptions

- Applicant Absent
- Examination Cancelled
- Invalid Submission
- Grading Error

---

# Notifications

- Examination Scheduled
- Reminder
- Results Published

---

# Result

Exam Result becomes available for Admission Decision.
