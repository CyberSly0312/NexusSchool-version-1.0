# Canteen Engine

## Purpose

Manages school cafeteria operations and meal services.

---

## Responsibilities

- Meal planning
- Menu management
- Meal distribution
- Student meal accounts
- Inventory tracking

---

## Inputs

- Students
- Meal Plans
- Inventory
- Payments

---

## Outputs

- Meal Reports
- Consumption Reports
- Inventory Reports

---

## Business Rules

- Meal eligibility verified.
- Stock cannot become negative.
- Daily menu scheduling.

---

## Produced Events

- MealServed
- MenuPublished
- InventoryUpdated

---

## Future Improvements

- QR meal verification
- Nutrition tracking
- Cashless cafeteria
