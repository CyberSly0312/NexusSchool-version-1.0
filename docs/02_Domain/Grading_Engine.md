# Grading Engine

## Purpose

Calculates grades and academic performance according to institutional grading policies.

---

## Responsibilities

- Calculate grades
- Compute GPA
- Compute CGPA
- Determine pass/fail
- Generate report card results

---

## Inputs

- Examination Scores
- Continuous Assessment
- Grading Scale

---

## Outputs

- Final Grade
- GPA
- CGPA
- Academic Standing

---

## Business Rules

- Institution grading scale enforced.
- Scores immutable after approval.
- GPA formula configurable.

---

## Produced Events

- GradesCalculated
- ResultsPublished

---

## Security

- Grade approval workflow
- Complete grading audit

---

## Future Improvements

- AI performance prediction
- Adaptive grading models
