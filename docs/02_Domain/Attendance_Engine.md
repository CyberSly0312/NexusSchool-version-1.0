# Attendance Engine

## Purpose

Coordinates attendance management for Students and Teachers.

---

## Responsibilities

- Record attendance
- Validate attendance
- Calculate attendance statistics
- Detect absenteeism
- Generate reports

---

## Inputs

- Student
- Teacher
- Timetable
- Academic Calendar

---

## Outputs

- Attendance Report
- Attendance Percentage
- Alerts

---

## Business Rules

- One attendance record per session.
- Late arrivals configurable.
- Excused absences supported.

---

## Produced Events

- AttendanceRecorded
- AttendanceValidated
- AttendanceAlertGenerated

---

## Future Improvements

- QR attendance
- Face recognition
- NFC attendance
