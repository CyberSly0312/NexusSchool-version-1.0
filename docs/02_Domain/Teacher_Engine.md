# Teacher Engine

## Purpose

Manages Teacher lifecycle and academic assignments.

---

## Responsibilities

- Teacher management
- Subject assignment
- Workload calculation
- Availability tracking
- Performance monitoring

---

## Inputs

- Teacher Profile
- Subjects
- Timetable
- Classes

---

## Outputs

- Workload Report
- Teaching Assignment
- Availability Report

---

## Business Rules

- Teachers cannot exceed workload limits.
- Subject qualifications enforced.
- Timetable conflicts prohibited.

---

## Produced Events

- TeacherAssigned
- TeacherTransferred
- WorkloadUpdated

---

## Future Improvements

- AI workload balancing
- Competency recommendations
