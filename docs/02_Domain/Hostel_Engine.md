# Hostel Engine

## Purpose

Manages student accommodation and hostel operations.

---

## Responsibilities

- Room allocation
- Bed assignment
- Occupancy management
- Hostel billing
- Maintenance requests

---

## Inputs

- Student Profiles
- Hostel Buildings
- Room Inventory

---

## Outputs

- Accommodation Records
- Occupancy Reports

---

## Business Rules

- One bed per student.
- Capacity limits enforced.
- Hostel fees validated.

---

## Produced Events

- RoomAssigned
- RoomVacated
- MaintenanceRequested

---

## Future Improvements

- Smart room management
- Visitor management
