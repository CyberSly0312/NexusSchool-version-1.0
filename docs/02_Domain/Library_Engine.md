# Library Engine

## Purpose

Coordinates library operations and resource management.

---

## Responsibilities

- Book catalog
- Borrowing
- Returns
- Reservations
- Fine calculation
- Inventory tracking

---

## Inputs

- Library Resources
- Student Accounts
- Staff Accounts

---

## Outputs

- Borrow Records
- Fine Reports
- Inventory Reports

---

## Business Rules

- Borrowing limits enforced.
- Overdue fines configurable.
- Lost items tracked.

---

## Produced Events

- BookBorrowed
- BookReturned
- FineGenerated

---

## Future Improvements

- RFID integration
- Digital library
- E-book support
