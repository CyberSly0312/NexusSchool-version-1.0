# Parent Engine

## Purpose

Manages Parents, Guardians and Student relationships.

---

## Responsibilities

- Parent profiles
- Guardian relationships
- Family grouping
- Student linking
- Emergency contacts

---

## Inputs

- Parent Profile
- Student Profile

---

## Outputs

- Family Structure
- Guardian Mapping

---

## Business Rules

- Multiple Guardians supported.
- One Parent may manage multiple Students.
- Emergency contacts required.

---

## Produced Events

- ParentRegistered
- GuardianAssigned

---

## Future Improvements

- Parent Portal
- Family Wallet
- Parent Mobile App
