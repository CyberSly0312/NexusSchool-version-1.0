# Communication Engine

## Purpose

Central communication platform for all system notifications.

---

## Responsibilities

- Email delivery
- SMS delivery
- Push notifications
- In-App messaging
- Announcement broadcasting

---

## Inputs

- Domain Events
- Templates
- User Preferences

---

## Outputs

- Notifications
- Delivery Reports

---

## Business Rules

- Notification preferences respected.
- Failed deliveries retried.
- Templates versioned.

---

## Produced Events

- NotificationSent
- NotificationFailed

---

## Future Improvements

- WhatsApp
- Telegram
- Voice calls
