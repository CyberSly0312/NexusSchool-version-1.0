# Soft Delete Strategy

## Purpose

Prevent accidental data loss while preserving historical information.

---

## Use Soft Deletes

- Students
- Teachers
- Parents
- Classes
- Subjects
- Programs

---

## Never Soft Delete

- Payments
- Audit Logs
- Academic Results
- Financial Transactions

---

## Restoration

Authorized administrators may restore soft deleted records.

---

## Audit

Every restore operation is logged.

---

## Business Rules

Deleted records remain excluded from normal queries unless explicitly requested.
