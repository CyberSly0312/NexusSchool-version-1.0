# ADR-006
# Staff Workforce Architecture

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-04

**Decision Makers:**

- Solution Architect
- Backend Architect
- HR / Domain Expert

---

# Context

A school's workforce is heterogeneous: teachers, directors, secretaries,
accountants, librarians and other employees. SPR-006 must model the people
behind these roles so that the school can:

- register and update professional identity (names, contact, national id, bank info);
- hire each person under **exactly one active employment contract**;
- assign them to departments, positions or campuses over time;
- record qualifications, certifications and emergency contacts;
- suspend, restore, transfer or terminate employment without losing the past;
- later pay them (Payroll), timetable their work (Timetable) and take
  attendance (Attendance).

History must be reliable and auditable: past contracts and assignments must
never be silently overwritten, and every workforce action must be traceable to
an actor.

SPR-001/002 (Academic), SPR-003 (Student), SPR-004 (Enrollment) and SPR-005
(Guardian) established the DDD + Actions structure, tenant isolation and
authorization conventions that this decision must follow.

---

# Problem Statement

How should the Staff domain model the workforce so that:

- **roles are never duplicated identities** — a Teacher is a Staff, not a
  separate record, so that a teacher who later becomes Director keeps one identity;
- a staff member has **exactly one active employment** per school;
- **assignments are time-aware** and never overlap;
- **transitions are controlled** and retired/resigned/terminated records are immutable;
- **history is append-only** and auditable;
- every action is **school-scoped** and **authorization-gated**;
- the presentation layer stays a thin consumer of the domain?

The model must not leak state transitions into the Eloquent model, and must not
allow the database to be bypassed by the Filament presentation layer.

---

# Decision

Nexus adopts a **single Staff aggregate root** whose role is a *specialization
value*, with an **append-only employment-history journal** and a **single
service facade** over explicit application actions.

```text
Staff (aggregate root, specialization: teacher | director | …)
   ├─ Employment (single active contract per school)
   ├─ EmploymentHistory (append-only journal)
   ├─ Assignment (department | position | campus, time-aware)
   ├─ Qualification / Certification
   ├─ StaffEmergencyContact
   └─ TeacherProfile (teaching subject ids)

StaffStatus: active <-> suspended -> terminated | retired | resigned (immutable)
```

- The `Staff` model is the aggregate root and holds **no transition logic**.
- Roles are a `StaffSpecialization` enum value on the record — never separate
  aggregates, never separate tables.
- All invariants and transitions live in **application Actions**, invoked only
  through the `StaffService` facade.
- **One active employment** per staff per school is enforced in the action and
  by a partial unique index `(school_id, staff_id)` where
  `termination_date IS NULL`.
- Every domain mutation dispatches a **domain event** consumed by a
  `StaffAuditLogger` listener, which writes to the `audit_logs` table and
  appends to the immutable `employment_history` journal.
- **Assignments** carry a start (and optional end) date with an `is_current`
  flag; the action rejects overlaps and cross-school targets.
- **Retired / resigned / terminated** staff are immutable (`isImmutable()`);
  deletions are soft-only, gated to `super_admin`.
- Authorization is centralized in `StaffPolicy` with 4 CRUD + 6 workforce
  permissions (`assign`, `transfer`, `terminate`, `suspend`, `restore`,
  `teach`); `super_admin` bypasses scoping.
- Staff numbers are `STF-{hex}-{seq}`, count-based per school, never re-used.

# Alternatives Considered

## Option 1

One persistent table per role (a `Teachers` table, a `Directors` table, …).

### Advantages

- Each role has a purpose-built schema.

### Disadvantages

- Duplicates the person record across roles.
- A teacher promoted to Director becomes two people.
- Cross-role reporting, payroll and attendance become join puzzles.
- Invariants (one active contract) cannot be enforced across tables.

---

## Option 2

Store professional history as denormalized snapshot fields on the Staff record.

### Advantages

- Simple to read.

### Disadvantages

- Loses the temporal structure of contracts and assignments.
- No single source of truth for "the current employment".
- History overwrites or drifts; audit/traceability impossible.

---

## Option 3 (Chosen)

Single aggregate root + specialization enum + append-only history journal.

### Advantages

- One identity per person for life.
- Exactly-one-active-employment is provable in DB and application layer.
- Assignments are first-class, time-aware records.
- History is immutable and auditable.
- Tenant isolation is applied on every query.
- Presentation stays thin behind the service facade.

### Disadvantages

- More tables and more code.
- Role-specific fields must live in supporting profiles
  (e.g. `TeacherProfile`) rather than on the root.
- Transition rules must be kept in the actions, not the model.

---

# Consequences

All workforce flows in NexusSchool now go through `StaffService`.

This concerns:

- Filament resources (Staff, Position, Employment, Assignment, Qualification,
  Certification) under "Ressources humaines"
- Authorization and permission definitions (`staff.*`)
- Future Payroll, Timetable and Attendance sprints, which consume Staff read-only

New workforce features must preserve the one-active-employment invariant and
the append-only journal.

---

# Positive Impacts

- Proven invariant: one active employment per staff member per school.
- Complete, auditable lifecycle with actor traceability.
- No silent history loss and no duplicated person identities.
- Tenant isolation enforced at query level.
- Testable, deterministic transitions (96 staff tests).

---

# Negative Impacts

- Larger schema and more moving parts than a flat model.
- Reading "current state" requires navigating multiple rows.
- Role-specific extensions require new supporting tables/profiles.

# Implementation Notes

Implemented under `app/Domains/Staff`:

- `Infrastructure/Models/Staff` — aggregate root (scopes, status helpers).
- `Application/Actions/*` — 10 explicit actions (create, update, employment,
  assignments, transfer, suspend/restore, terminate, teaching subjects).
- `Application/Services/StaffService` — the only public entry point.
- `Domain/Enums/StaffStatus` / `StaffSpecialization` — the state machine and roles.
- `Domain/Events/*` + `Infrastructure/Listeners/StaffAuditLogger` — audit + history.
- `Infrastructure/Policies/StaffPolicy` — permission-gated authorization.
- `StaffServiceProvider` — observers, policy, event bindings, singletons.

Schema: 9 migrations (staffs, positions, employments, employment_history,
assignments, qualifications, certifications, staff_emergency_contacts,
teacher_profiles).
