# ADR-021
# Certificate & Transcript Engine Architecture

**Status:** Proposed

**Date:** 2026-09-05

**Decision Makers:**

- Solution Architect
- Backend Architect
- Academic / Domain Expert

---

# Context

NexusSchool must issue official academic completion documents — academic
transcripts (relevés de notes) and end-of-study certificates (certificats) —
while preserving a permanent, auditable academic memory.

The platform already owns the pieces this needs:

- **SPR-010 Gradebook** validates and freezes per-term grades (locked /
  published gradebooks, immutable grade entries with coefficient, average,
  rank, grade point, letter grade, appreciation).
- **SPR-011 Report Card** composes an immutable per-term snapshot
  (`report_card_entries.content`) from validated upstream values — verbatim,
  never recalculated — and links each entry to the published
  `AcademicDocument` (`academic_document_id`, `academic_document_number`).
- **SPR-013 Class Council** is the single academic-validation layer; only
  published decisions are visible to other engines.
- **SPR-012 Academic Communication** is the single publication layer
  (ADR-012): it owns numbering, templates, versions, content hashes,
  publication policies, PDF rendering and delivery. Its `DocumentType` already
  declares `transcript` (code `TR`) and `certificate` (code `CE`).
- **SPR-014 Records Lifecycle** registers every document/council/report-card
  record in an append-only journal.
- **SPR-016 Identity & Verification** (future) will own QR/public verification.

The Discovery phase (SPR-015) concluded `NEEDS ARCHITECTURAL DECISION` and
identified six unresolved decisions. This ADR resolves them so that SPR-015 can
later be implemented without re-arguing architecture.

The governing boundary is immutable (`docs/docs/SESSION_HANDOFF.md` § 8):

> **Business engines never generate PDFs.**
> **Academic Communication is the single publication layer.**
> **Published documents are immutable; corrections create new versions.**

---

# Problem Statement

How should the Certificate & Transcript Engine (SPR-015) be structured so that:

- it does **not** create a parallel PDF/document pipeline and stays compatible
  with SPR-012;
- transcripts remain **historically reproducible** — an already-issued
  transcript must not change when academic configuration (subject names,
  coefficients, re-grades) changes years later;
- certificate eligibility comes from **authoritative, published** evidence and
  is never recomputed independently, avoiding competing sources of truth;
- enrollment / graduation facts (level, graduation date) reach the engine
  without leaking foreign models across domain boundaries;
- permissions follow the per-engine capability model and least privilege;
- tenant isolation, events, and auditability match existing engine
  conventions.

---

# Decision

SPR-015 is a **dedicated business engine** in `app/Domains/CertificateAndTranscript/`
that follows the SPR-011 Report Card pattern: it **validates and composes**
academic completion documents from published evidence, persists its own
immutable content snapshot, and **delegates every document to SPR-012** through
a contract adapter. It adds **no** PDF pipeline, **no** numbering, **no**
template logic, and **no** publication route of its own.

The six decisions are declared below. For each: `DECISION:`, `RATIONALE:`,
`CONSEQUENCES:`, `ALTERNATIVES REJECTED:`.

---

## Decision 1 — Architectural Boundary

```
DECISION:
SPR-015 is a dedicated domain (Option A): app/Domains/CertificateAndTranscript/.

It owns:
- certificate / transcript eligibility verdicts (orchestrated from evidence);
- the immutable academic completion snapshot (transcript / certificate content);
- orchestration of published sources (SPR-011 snapshots, SPR-013 decisions,
  Academic Policies, Enrollment / Graduation facts);
- its own per-domain contracts (Gradebook / ReportCard / ClassCouncil /
  AcademicPolicies / StudentIdentity / AcademicCommunication) with
  Infrastructure adapters bound in its own ServiceProvider;
- Capability-based authorization via a dedicated permission group (Decision 6).

It does NOT own:
- document numbering, templates, versions, publication policies, rendering or
  delivery — those remain exclusively with SPR-012;
- PDF rendering of any kind;
- calculation of grades or standing (SPR-010 / SPR-011 authority).

RATIONALE:
SPR-011 is the existing, working precedent: it is a self-contained engine with
its own snapshot aggregate, per-domain contracts, architecture tests, and an
AcademicCommunicationContract adapter as the only seam to SPR-012
(app/Domains/ReportCard/Infrastructure/Contracts/AcademicCommunicationAdapter.php).
SPR-013 and SPR-010 follow the same per-engine shape. This preserves the
acyclic dependency graph that the architecture tests enforce, keeps business
rules (eligibility) out of the document engine, and avoids making SPR-012's
already large surface the home of yet more business logic.

CONSEQUENCES:
- New domain + new contract set + an AcademicCommunicationContract adapter
  (mirrors SPR-011's generate/publish seams: generateTranscriptDocument,
  generateCertificateDocument + publish).
- `DocumentOrigin` must gain additive cases (Transcript, Certificate) so the
  adapter can tag the SPR-012 document with its canonical origin — the enum
  currently has no such cases. This is an additive, non-behavioral change made
  during SPR-015 implementation.
- SPR-011 / SPR-013 / SPR-012 / SPR-010 / SPR-014 behavior is untouched.

ALTERNATIVES REJECTED:
- Option B (all logic inside SPR-012 as GenerateTranscriptAction /
  GenerateCertificateAction): puts eligibility business rules and snapshot
  responsibilities into the document engine, inverting the
  "business engines vs. document engine" boundary; would force SPR-012
  contracts to grow into every academic source. Rejected.
- Option C (hybrid: validation in SPR-015, composition in SPR-012): viable but
  splits ownership of the content snapshot across two engines and costs the
  benefits of the single, precedent-setting SPR-011 shape. Rejected for
  complexity; can be revisited only via ADR superseding this one.
```

## Decision 2 — Transcript Source of Truth

```
DECISION:
The authoritative historical source for transcripts is the set of PUBLISHED
Report Card snapshots (Option B): the immutable `report_card_entries.content`
rows of the student's retained academic history, each traced to its published
SPR-012 document via `academic_document_id` / `academic_document_number`.

A transcript is assembled by reading those content snapshots verbatim —
student, period (year/term/class/section), gradebook rows (subject id/name/
code, average, percentage, coefficient, grade_point, letter_grade,
appreciation, rank, rank_denominator), standing (gpa, cgpa,
overall_percentage, academic_standing, failed_subjects), attendance, policies
and council verdict (see ReportCardContentComposer::compose()).

SPR-015 persists the assembled transcript as its own immutable snapshot
(content JSON + the academic_document_number citation) at issuance time,
mirroring report_card_entries.

RATIONALE:
`report_card_entries.content` is serialized once, at generation time, from
validated upstream values and never recomputed (ReportCardContentComposer
docblock: "The engine never performs any calculation"). Its published
counterpart in SPR-012 is immutable (DocumentStatus::isImmutable() on
published/archived; ADR-012). Therefore a transcript built from these
snapshots CANNOT change retroactively when subject names, coefficients, or
grading configuration change after issuance. Recomputation from gradebooks
would be vulnerable to coefficient re-use, subject rename/soft-delete, and the
absence of a persisted standings table (AcademicStandingService recomputes on
demand).

CONSEQUENCES:
- Transcripts are only issuable for academic history that produced published
  report cards. Terms without a published report card are excluded; an
  issuance request covering unpublished terms must be refused or explicitly
  marked partial (decided at implementation under the same integrity rule).
- A students who were never in the report-card flow (edge cases) cannot get a
  transcript from snapshots; fallback recomposition from locked/published
  gradebooks only is permitted and must be recorded as a distinctly-origined
  snapshot, never merged with snapshot-derived data.

ALTERNATIVES REJECTED:
- Option A (recompose from published/locked gradebooks): grades are fixed on
  lock, which is strong, but standing/GPA is recomputed on demand and subject
  to changed coefficients and configuration; "what was published as the
  official result" is not the same as "what the current config computes".
  Rejected as the primary source.
- Option C (academic_document_versions.content as the base): those contents
  are SPR-012's own composed views of the same data; reading them directly
  would couple SPR-015 to SPR-012's internal document content contracts.
  Rejected; the citation (academic_document_number) is kept instead.
```

## Decision 3 — Verification / QR Boundary

```
DECISION:
SPR-015 does NOT generate QR codes and does NOT implement verification.

- SPR-015 supplies only verification-ready metadata: the permanent SPR-012
  document number (the natural verification handle) and any verification-code
  placeholder carried in the composed content for the template.
- SPR-012 remains the carrier: `AcademicDocumentTemplate.qr_code_enabled` /
  `verification_code_enabled` and the permanently numbered, immutable
  document are the verification substrate today (the renderer already passes
  these flags to the view).
- SPR-016 (Identity & Verification Engine, future) owns QR encoding, public
  verification endpoints, and identity verification. It consumes the SPR-012
  document identity/metadata produced above.

RATIONALE:
No QR library exists in the project; the view currently prints only the
document number as "code de vérification"; roadmap assigns verification to
SPR-016. Pre-implementing verification in SPR-015 would duplicate the future
engine and inflate scope. Using the immutable, permanent document number as
the verification handle now costs nothing and is fully compatible with SPR-016.

CONSEQUENCES:
- SPR-015 ships no QR dependency and no verification endpoints.
- Certificate/transcript templates that demand a QR will surface a visible
  gap until SPR-016; documented as expected, not a defect.

ALTERNATIVES REJECTED:
- Generating QR in SPR-012: outside the single-verification-engine boundary,
  adds dependencies to the doc engine. Rejected.
- Embedding a self-verifying hash in the SPR-015 content: unnecessary today
  (content_hash already exists on SPR-012 versions) and pre-empts SPR-016.
  Rejected.
```

## Decision 4 — Certificate Eligibility

```
DECISION:
Certificate eligibility is a VERDICT orchestrated by SPR-015 from authoritative,
published evidence — never recomputed, never competing.

Evidence sources (fixed, non-negotiable):
1. SPR-013 published Class Council decision for the final period (Passed /
   orientation) — the academic truth (ADR-013); accessed read-only via SPR-015's
   ClassCouncilContract (only published decisions, mirroring `ClassCouncilContract::publishedDecisions`).
2. Academic Policies (AcademicPoliciesContract, per-school key/value) for any
   threshold / criterion — eligibility thresholds are policy-driven, never
   hardcoded (passing % lives in config/policies today, e.g. class-council
   passing percentage).
3. EnrollmentGraduation record when present — the authoritative graduation
   verdict, graduation date, and education level (immutable history).
4. Published Report Card snapshot(s) — the supporting academic record
   (Decision 2).

Ownership:
- Decides: SPR-015 (single eligibility authority for completion documents).
- Provides evidence: SPR-013 (decisions), SPR-011 (snapshots), Academic
  Policies (thresholds), Enrollment (graduation record).
- Validates: SPR-015 consumes only published / immutable evidence; if any
  required evidence is unpublished (e.g. draft council session), SPR-015
  refuses issuance.
- Composes: SPR-015 builds the certificate content snapshot.
- Publishes: SPR-012.

The eligibility verdict and its evidence citations are persisted in SPR-015's
own snapshot for auditability.

RATIONALE:
ADR-013 declares the council decision "the academic truth the rest of the
platform must respect"; a graduation record is canonical for graduation facts;
Academic Policies is the engine contract for school-configurable rules. SPR-015
must aggregate, not invent. This prevents the classic failure of a second,
disagreeing "pass/fail" calculation living in the certificate code.

CONSEQUENCES:
- SPR-015 introduces no new decision authority; a hardened "fail" certificate
  cannot appear without a matching published council decision or graduation
  record, because issuance is gated on those.
- School-specific criteria remain configuration (policies), so no per-school
  code forks.

ALTERNATIVES REJECTED:
- Hardcoding a passing threshold in SPR-015: violates the policy-driven rule
  (Academic Policies exists for exactly this). Rejected.
- Letting SPR-013 compute eligibility: SPR-013 decides academic validation;
  it does not own "completion document eligibility", which unifies council +
  graduation + level. Rejected; SPR-013 stays untouched.
```

## Decision 5 — Enrollment / Graduation Contract

```
DECISION:
YES — SPR-015 requires a read-only contract for enrollment/graduation facts.

It is OWNED BY SPR-015: `App\Domains\CertificateAndTranscript\Domain\Contracts\EnrollmentContract`
(per-engine convention — Gradebook, ClassCouncil, ReportCard, Attendance,
Assessment each define their own EnrollmentContract), implemented by an SPR-015
Infrastructure `EnrollmentAdapter` bound in its own ServiceProvider. It exposes
model-free value objects only (e.g. an `EnrollmentReference` / `GraduationReference`).

Minimum information it provides (composition-grade facts over a student's
retained history):
- enrollment periods (academic year / term / class / section);
- education level of the relevant enrollment;
- graduation verdict + graduation date + certificate reference when the
  enrollment is closed by graduation.

It must NOT expose:
- mutation surface of the Enrollment domain (no writes, no transitions);
- internal raw notes, deliberation detail, or unpublished state;
- student PII beyond what the existing StudentIdentityContract already
  provides (name, student number, class, section).

RATIONALE:
EnrollmentGraduation (decision, graduated_on, certificate_reference,
education_level_id) is the authoritative "immutable history" for graduation.
Every engine already consumes enrollment through its own read-only contract —
there is no shared cross-domain enrollment contract, and direct model access
would violate the acyclic dependency and architecture-test rules.

CONSEQUENCES:
- Small additional contract + adapter inside SPR-015; no change to the
  Enrollment domain and no change to any other engine's EnrollmentContract.
- Read-only by construction (contract returns value objects; adapter queries
  with school scoping only).

ALTERNATIVES REJECTED:
- Reusing ReportCard's or Gradebook's EnrollmentContract: contracts are
  engine-local by design; cross-engine reuse couples SPR-015 to another
  engine's contract surface. Rejected.
- Direct Eloquent use of EnrollmentGraduation inside SPR-015: violates the
  boundary architecture tests enforce. Rejected.
```

## Decision 6 — Permissions

```
DECISION:
Option B — a dedicated permission group `CertificateAndTranscript`,
capability-based (never role-based), following the per-engine precedent
(ReportCard -> generate/regenerate/publish; ClassCouncil -> convene/decide/publish/archive;
AcademicCommunication 'document' -> view/generate/review/approve/publish/archive/template.manage).

Capabilities at minimum: view, generate, review, approve, publish, archive.
Verification capabilities are deliberately reserved for SPR-016's group, not
created here. A future post-publish "revoke" capability is reserved but not
activated in SPR-015.

RATIONALE:
Project precedent (PermissionGroup.Send in Enum and PermissionService) gives
every engine its own group; reusing the generic `document` group would blur
responsibility and cannot express certificate-specific auditability. Least
privilege is preserved by separating review from approve from publish.

CONSEQUENCES:
- New PermissionGroup + seeder additions within SPR-015 implementation;
  existing groups untouched.
- School Admin can delegate transcript/certificate issuance to Secretary/
  Director without widening document.* powers.

ALTERNATIVES REJECTED:
- Option A (reuse `document` group): cannot express distinct lifecycle stages
  or audit certificate operations separately; couples SPR-015 roles to
  document-template management. Rejected.
- Option C (hybrid reuse + a few extras): muddles the model. Rejected.

# Architectural Invariants

These invariants are binding for SPR-015 implementation and review.

### A

Business engines do not render PDFs. SPR-015 never produces a PDF itself.

### B

SPR-012 remains the document publication/rendering boundary. SPR-015 always
reaches it through a contract adapter.

### C

Published academic documents are immutable (`DocumentStatus::isImmutable()`).
SPR-015 never mutates a published SPR-012 document; corrections become new
versions/documents via SPR-012.

### D

Historical corrections are forward-only. SPR-015 builds on published snapshots
plus its own new snapshot; it never rewrites the historical academic record.

### E

Cross-domain access happens only through approved contracts/adapters, one
engine per boundary (archived-code style, enforced by per-domain architecture
tests).

### F

Tenant-owned records remain school-scoped. Every SPR-015 model/query uses
`HasSchoolId` + `scopeForSchool`; document files stay tenant-isolated under
SPR-012's `documents/{school_id}/...` path.

### G

Certificate eligibility comes from authoritative published evidence (council
decision, graduation record, policy thresholds, report-card snapshots). SPR-015
introduces no competing calculation authority.

### H

Verification mechanics stay within the correct boundary: SPR-016 owns
QR/public verification; SPR-015 supplies only verification-ready metadata.

### I

SPR-015 does not duplicate existing sources of truth (no second grade
calculation, no second council decision, no second document pipeline).

### J

Legacy document infrastructure is not silently reintroduced (see Legacy
Document System below).

# Scope

SPR-015 WILL own:

- `app/Domains/CertificateAndTranscript/` domain (Application actions +
  services, Domain contracts + references, Infrastructure models + adapters +
  policies, ServiceProvider, architecture tests) mirroring SPR-011's layout.
- Transcript issuance composed from published Report Card snapshots
  (Decision 2) with its own immutable completion snapshot.
- End-of-study certificate issuance gated on published council decision +
  graduation record + policy thresholds (Decision 4).
- Its own `EnrollmentContract` + adapter (Decision 5).
- A dedicated `CertificateAndTranscript` permission group and capabilities
  (Decision 6).
- Delegation of all document generation/publication to SPR-012 via an
  `AcademicCommunicationContract` adapter, including the additive
  `DocumentOrigin` Transcript/Certificate cases required by the adapter.
- Events + audit emission consistent with other engines; lifecycle
  registration through SPR-014's contract.
- Tests: lifecycle, policy, tenant isolation, records-lifecycle integration,
  architecture boundary tests (Pest, following existing conventions).

## Non-Goals

- QR generation and public verification (SPR-016).
- Identity verification (SPR-016).
- Direct PDF rendering (SPR-012).
- Modification of published documents (SPR-012 forward-only versions).
- Changes to SPR-011 behavior, contracts, or data.
- Changes to SPR-013 behavior, contracts, or data.
- Changes to SPR-012 behavior or its existing contracts/types beyond the
  additive `DocumentOrigin` cases required by the adapter.
- Changes to SPR-014 status or content (see discrepancy note below).
- Retirement or rewrite of the legacy document system (future work only; see
  below).
- Attestations / enrollment confirmations / official letters beyond the
  academic completion scope (transcript + end-of-study certificate) — those are
  SPR-012 generic/official paths, not SPR-015.

# Known Architectural / Governance Discrepancy — SPR-014

Governance documentation states SPR-014 **(Records Lifecycle)** as DEFERRED /
PLANNED / NOT STARTED, while the repository contains a substantial
implementation: `app/Domains/RecordsLifecycle/`, migration
`2026_08_10_000000_create_record_lifecycles_table.php`,
`RecordsLifecycleContract` implemented in ReportCard, ClassCouncil and
AcademicCommunication, integration tests, and a SPR-014 engine report.

This ADR acknowledges the discrepancy as a **Known Architectural / Governance
Discrepancy**.

It does **not** resolve it: SPR-014 status is not changed here, its code is not
touched, and its report is not rewritten.

**Follow-up Action** (out of scope): the Architecture Board must reconcile
governance status (DEFERRED vs. implemented) — after which SPR-015 lifecycle
integration requirements can be finalized accordingly.

Operationally, SPR-015's transcript/certificate documents will register
lifecycle records through the SPR-012 generate path if that engine's
integration is active — consistent with how report cards behave today.

# Legacy Document System

The repository contains an older document architecture:

- `app/Models/GeneratedDocument`, `app/Models/DocumentTemplate`
- `app/Services/DocumentGenerationService`
- `app/Domains/Document/Enums/DocumentType` (SchoolCertificate,
  EnrollmentAttestation, AttendanceAttestation, ReportCard, Transcript,
  SchoolId, EndOfStudyCertificate, TransferLetter, CustomCertificate)
- Filament Director `DocumentCenter` page and `resources/views/documents/*`
  static template mockups (not referenced from any engine code; legacy random
  `STR-` reference numbering conflicts with SPR-012's permanent numbering).

DECISION: SPR-015 **excludes** the legacy system. It neither uses, wraps, nor
migrates it. SPR-012's `DocumentType` (with `transcript`/`certificate`) and its
permanent numbering are the only document substrate.

Legacy retirement (aligning/retiring `GeneratedDocument`/`DocumentTemplate`/
`DocumentGenerationService` and the static views) is recorded as **future
work**, outside this ADR's authorization, so that no second document pipeline is
reintroduced.

# Alternatives Rejected (Summary)

- **Per-engine documents** (each engine renders its own): rejected by ADR-012;
  numbering/versioning/visibility would fragment (ADR-012 Option 1).
- **Generic blob document store**: rejected by ADR-012 (Option 2); no
  lifecycle/immutability.
- **All SPR-015 logic inside SPR-012** (Decision 1, Option B): inverts the
  business/document engine boundary.
- **Hybrid SPR-015 + SPR-012 composition** (Decision 1, Option C): splits
  snapshot ownership; rejected for complexity.
- **Gradebook recomposition as primary transcript source** (Decision 2,
  Option A): vulnerable to config drift; no persisted standings.
- **`academic_document_versions.content` as transcript base** (Decision 2,
  Option C): couples SPR-015 to SPR-012 internals.
- **QR in SPR-012 or self-hash in SPR-015** (Decision 3): duplicates SPR-016.
- **Hardcoded eligibility thresholds** (Decision 4): violates policy-driven
  rule.
- **Reusing another engine's EnrollmentContract** (Decision 5): engines keep
  private contract surfaces.
- **Direct Eloquent access to EnrollmentGraduation** (Decision 5): violates
  domain-boundary rules.
- **Reusing the `document` permission group** (Decision 6, Option A): cannot
  express certificate lifecycle or audit distinctly.

Consequences of rejection: each rejected option is preserved above so future
maintainers can re-consider a path only through a superseding ADR — never by
silent drift.

# Consequences

## Positive

- Clean domain boundaries: SPR-015 is a thin, testable validator/composer,
  consistent with SPR-011/SPR-013.
- Historical integrity: transcripts/certificates are reproducible from
  published snapshots and immutable documents; late config changes cannot
  rewrite issued documents.
- Reuse: numbering, templates, hashing, rendering, publication and lifecycle
  come from SPR-012/SPR-014 — no duplicated pipeline to maintain.
- Policy-driven eligibility, tenant-safe by construction (HasSchoolId +
  scopeForSchool on every model and query).
- Future extensibility: additional completion document kinds slot in as new
  SPR-015 orchestrations + SPR-012 types/origins, without touching engines.
- Testing aligned with existing suites (lifecycle, policy, tenant isolation,
  records-lifecycle integration, architecture boundary).

## Negative

- Additional contracts/adapters (including the SPR-015-owned EnrollmentContract
  and AcademicCommunicationContract).
- Orchestration complexity and snapshot strategy complexity (Decision 2) — the
  composer must aggregate multiple published snapshots and refuse unpublished
  evidence.
- Permission administration grows (new group + capabilities seeded per
  tenant).
- Legacy retirement remains pending (documented future work, not done here).
- SPR-016 verification gap is visible on templates that want a QR code until
  that engine lands.
